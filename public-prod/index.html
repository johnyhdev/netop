Frequently Asked Questions

Common Queries About Netop Lab Projects

Clear answers regarding usage, licensing, operational costs, and extension capabilities.

Are these projects free to use and commercialize?

Most codebases are released under the MIT License, allowing you to adapt them freely. However, you should review third-party tool binaries (like Terraform) and software dependencies, and consult legal experts before commercial packaging.

How can I manage and minimize cloud costs during testing?

While running VMs, disks, or egress bandwidth incur provider costs, the labs include automated destroy workflows to tear down infrastructure completely after testing, keeping expenses minimal when controlled tightly.

How can I support Netop Lab or contribute?
