Privacy Policy

Overview & Scope

At Netop Lab (https://netop.uk/), I respect your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, process, and safeguard information when you browse my site, use my technical guides, or interact with my communication channels.

This policy applies to all visitors globally, with specific protections for residents of the European Union (EU/EEA) under GDPR and United States (US) under CCPA/CPRA.

Information I Collect

I collect minimal data necessary to maintain site security, technical performance, and user communication:

Data Category Examples / Details Collection Purpose
Technical Data Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, OS, referral URLs. Performance analysis, DDoS protection, traffic logs.
Communication Data Name, email address, and chat logs provided via contact forms or live chat. Answering technical queries and support.
Usage & Interaction Pages viewed, link clicks, time spent per session. Content optimization and system stability.

AI Data Processing & Automated Systems

I leverage advanced infrastructure automation and AI-assisted tools to improve technical documentation and site features.

  • No Automated Profiling: I do not use your personal data for automated profiling, behavioral advertising targeting, or credit scoring.
  • Input Data Protection: Any code snippets, logs, or text submitted to us via contact forms or live chat are scrubbed of sensitive identifiers before being processed by internal AI troubleshooting tools.
  • No Selling of AI Training Sets: I never sell or license visitor interaction data to third-party AI model providers.

Live Chat Privacy (Tawk.to) for EU & US Residents

I use Tawk.to to provide real-time messaging support. I have configured Tawk.to with strict privacy parameters to comply with GDPR (EU) and CCPA/CPRA (US) standards:

  • EU/EEA Visitors (GDPR): Chat features respect user consent settings. IPs are partially anonymized, and tracking cookies are only placed upon explicit user interaction or consent.
  • US Residents (CCPA/CPRA): I do not sell, rent, or share personal information gathered via live chat logs for commercial cross-context advertising.
  • Data Access & Erasure: You may request a full export or complete deletion of your live chat transcripts at any time by contacting us.

Affiliate Links & External Tracking

netop.uk participates in technical affiliate networks (e.g., Cloud providers, SaaS platforms, hardware tools).

  • How Affiliate Links Work: Clicking an affiliate link redirects you to a third-party vendor. A temporary affiliate cookie may be stored on your browser to attribute sales and reward netop.uk with a referral commission at no additional cost to you.
  • No Personal Data Exposure: I do not transmit your name, IP address, or payment credentials to affiliate partners.
  • Third-Party Policies: Once you leave netop.uk, your interactions are governed by the respective vendor’s privacy policy.

Regulatory Compliance (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA, you hold the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your stored personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request permanent deletion of your data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how I process your personal records.

United States Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

US visitors have the right to know what data is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of any potential data transfers. I explicitly confirm: I do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention & Security Measures

  • Zero Public VM Access: My backend architecture operates on Zero-Trust access model via Tailscale mesh network with OIDC authentication.
  • Retention Policy: Server access logs and chat transcripts are periodically purged after 30 days unless legally required to retain longer.
  • Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.2 - 1.3 encryption.

Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact me: Contact Me
