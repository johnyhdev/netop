## Transparency & Engineering Integrity

At **Netop Lab** (`netop.uk`), transparency and trust are core values. To support the maintenance, hosting infrastructure, and ongoing research for my open-source tools, technical guides, and **EVE-NG** lab architectures, some links on this website are **affiliate links**.

This means that if you click on an external product link and make a purchase or subscribe to a service, I may receive a referral commission at **no additional cost to you**.

---

## My Hands-On Recommendation Philosophy

I follow a strict **"Tested in Virtualized Labs"** policy before sharing any link or tool:

- **No Sponsored Hype**: I never accept payment to write positive reviews or recommend products that I have not personally deployed, tested, or validated in my **EVE-NG** virtualized lab environments.
- **Strict Relevance**: Every affiliate link strictly aligns with my core focus areas: Cloud Infrastructure (Azure, Cloudflare), VPS providers, Domain Registrars, Zero-Trust Tools (Tailscale), and specialized Network Automation tooling.
- **Cost Neutrality**: Purchasing a product or service through an affiliate link will **never increase the price you pay**. In many cases, using my referral links or promotion codes may offer special discounts or extended trial periods.

---

## How Affiliate Links Work

When you click an affiliate link on `netop.uk`:

1. Your browser is redirected to the official vendor or marketplace (Cloud Providers, Domain Registrars, SaaS Platforms).
2. A temporary, anonymized cookie is saved on your browser by the vendor to attribute the referral to Netop Lab.
3. **Zero Personal Data Exposure**: I do **not** collect, store, or process your billing details, credit card numbers, or personal identity during affiliate transactions. All purchases occur directly on the vendor's secure system.
